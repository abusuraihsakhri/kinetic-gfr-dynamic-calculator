Not a treatment decision rule. Kinetic GFR is an estimate, not measured clearance or KDIGO AKI staging. Interpret in clinical context; fluid shifts and changing creatinine generation can invalidate the assumptions.

Method and limitations

KeGFR = (SCr steady × GFR steady / mean SCr) × [1 − 24 × (SCr₂ − SCr₁) / (hours × max rise/day)]

Baseline GFR or creatinine clearance must correspond to the entered steady-state creatinine. The result retains the baseline estimate's normalization: do not treat an indexed eGFR (mL/min/1.73 m²) as absolute mL/min for dosing without appropriate assessment. The maximum anuric rise defaults to an illustrative 1.5 mg/dL/day; it varies between patients. Impossible negative estimates are rejected instead of presented as physiologic.

Reference: Chen S. Retooling the creatinine clearance equation to estimate kinetic GFR when the plasma creatinine is changing acutely. J Am Soc Nephrol. 2013;24:877–888. PMID 23704286.
